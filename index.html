The opportunity

A matched pair of Thorp T-211 S-LSA aircraft.

Both aircraft have current registrations in the seller’s name and S-LSA Certificates of Airworthiness. Both were disassembled for transport and are now located together in Lebanon, Illinois.

The package is best suited to an experienced aircraft owner, A&P, LSA repairman, builder, or buyer who wants one aircraft with a running Jabiru 3300 and a second aircraft with Dynon avionics and original wings and landing gear.
